# ClimbTheWorld-Android

Rock climbing frontend for OpenStreetMap.

* Download the latest APK: https://github.com/xyz-relativity/ClimbTheWorld-Android/releases/latest
* Support us on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/climb_the_world?fan_landing=true

## Augmented reality view

Point the camera at the rock to see the climbing areas, crags and routes from OpenStreetMap where
they are, with a map of what is in view underneath.

|                                    ![A.R. view of a cliff from the approach](pics/ar-cliff-from-approach.png)                                     |        ![A.R. view of the same cliff from the road](pics/ar-cliff-from-road.png)         |                  ![A.R. view close to a crag](pics/ar-crag-routes.png)                   |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|
| From the approach: each crag of the cliff with its route count, the climbing area outlined in cyan, and the virtual terrain following the ground. | The same cliff from the road below, with the crags and routes placed at their elevation. | Close to a crag: each route shows its grade and name, with the crags outlined in yellow. |
